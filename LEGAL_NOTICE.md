## 1. Statutory Protections

### Consumer Review Fairness Act (CRFA)
Under the **United States Consumer Review Fairness Act (15 U.S.C. § 45b)**, corporate entities are strictly prohibited from utilizing non-disparagement clauses, "threats of litigation," or terms of service provisions to restrict, penalize, or suppress honest, non-commercial assessments, reviews, or performance data of their software, billing practices, or service execution. Any attempt to force the removal of consumer-submitted transaction evidence via automated threat letters is void under federal law.

### Fair Use Affirmation (17 U.S.C. § 107)
All corporate designations, primary logos, interface configurations, and verbatim quotes utilized in this documentation are explicitly integrated under **Fair Use doctrines**. This deployment is entirely transformative and serves public interest functions specifically protected under statutory exceptions:
* **News Reporting & Whistleblowing:** Documenting active regulatory filings and administrative proceedings.
* **Criticism & Commentary:** Dissecting technical performance metrics, latency dependencies, and architectural claims.
* **Research & Archiving:** Securing public access to consumer records or testimony that have faced systemic commercial deletion patterns.
